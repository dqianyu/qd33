Measuring health where data are sparse

I’m a Ph.D. candidate in Statistics at UC Santa Cruz, advised by Zehang Richard Li. I build statistical models for public health, drawing on Bayesian methods, survey statistics, and small area estimation. My current project uses cross-fitting to evaluate models that estimate prevalence from complex surveys.
